# Collect company context

Maintain the company facts that research and outreach need. Read the
[research-partner workflow](../_shared/research-partner-workflow.md) and locate the
company's canonical dashboard. Reuse it; create one from the directory's default
template only when none exists.

Read the existing Company Brain, Library, call notes, and approved public sources
before asking questions. Capture the current offer and pricing, market position,
customer needs and examples, goals and baseline, sales process, and responsible
team members. Save the team's outreach voice and first-message rules under Sales
process & channels. Ask only about gaps or conflicts that materially affect research,
outreach, or measurement.

Use the seven Company Brain headings defined in the workflow contract. Record
current facts, distinguish plans from capabilities and targets from results, and
date time-sensitive claims. Keep original sources in Library. Mark facts that
still need human confirmation in red; do not invent or pad missing information.

The Company Brain describes the company. Observable targeting rules belong in
Segments; review results and refinements belong with Segments and Lists. Keep
multiple goals on the same company dashboard with consistent names.

Preserve useful facts, sources, and human edits. Read back the saved page and
report material additions, conflicts, and remaining confirmations.
