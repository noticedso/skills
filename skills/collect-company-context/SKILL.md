# Collect company context

Maintain the concise company source of truth that research and outreach agents use.
Company facts live in the Company Brain; targeting rules and batch learning do not.

Read the [research-partner workflow](../_shared/research-partner-workflow.md).
Start from the partner directory, dashboard, or company page supplied in the
conversation. Resolve the canonical partner record and connected noticed team.
When a directory is available, reuse a matching partner; if absent, create it from
that directory's default template. Do not use a separate staging page.

## Gather the useful facts

Read the existing dashboard, Company Brain, Library, call notes, and approved
public sources before asking the user to repeat information. Establish the
company's current offer, business goals, measurable baseline, deadline, existing
sales process, and the team members responsible.

Ask one focused question at a time only when the answer materially changes
research, outreach, or success measurement. Prefer concise grounded options when
they help, and allow free-form corrections. Resolve material source conflicts
with the user.

## Maintain the Company Brain

Keep exactly these seven headings:

1. Company & product
2. Pricing & business model
3. Market & positioning
4. Customer needs & examples
5. Business goals & baseline
6. Sales process & channels
7. Team & responsibilities

Capture current facts that help an agent understand what the company does and how
it works. Include only currently available offers and pricing. Distinguish
capabilities from plans and actual results from targets. Date baselines and
time-sensitive claims.

Keep original source links in the collapsed **Library** toggle and cite a source
beside a fact when its provenance materially affects confidence. Mark text that
still needs human confirmation in red. Do not pad empty sections or invent
missing facts.

The Company Brain describes the company. Observable targeting rules belong in
Segments, and reviews belong in Lists and the segment's learning log. Do not add
ICP prose, target-profile definitions, or hypothesis pages here. A company may
have several goals; keep them on the same dashboard with consistent names rather
than creating duplicate company pages.

Preserve useful existing facts, sources, and human edits. Read back the saved
content, then report the meaningful additions, conflicts, and facts still needing
confirmation. Keep partner-specific information in its workspace, never in this
skill.
