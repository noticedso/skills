# Collect company context

Build the company reference that agents will use to propose 3 target profiles, identify opportunities and prepare outreach.

1. Understand the company’s current strategy and the engagement’s stated goal. Adapt how you gather context to the situation, prioritizing direct evidence. Ask only questions that materially change targeting, fit, outreach or success measurement.

2. Fill the section toggles inside **Knowledge → Company context**:
   - Company
   - Product & offer
   - Market & positioning
   - ICP & target profiles
   - Business goals & baseline
   - Sales approach
   - Team & responsibilities

3. Anchor three proposed target profiles to the engagement’s goal: two ICP tracks and one connector track. ICP tracks may represent customers, investors or another relevant audience. Explain the evidence and reasoning behind each. Confirm them with the customer before treating them as agreed.

4. Distinguish available capabilities from plans, actual results from targets, and facts from assumptions. Date baselines and time-sensitive claims; flag unresolved source conflicts. Store supporting materials in Library and link evidence beside facts when useful.

5. Aim for a 600–800-word reference containing only context that changes who to target, why they fit, how to approach them or how success is measured. Do not pad missing information. Preserve the template’s layout and toggles, add the updated date, read back the saved content and summarize remaining decisions.

This is the current company reference, not a learning log. Keep customer-specific information outside this skill.
