# Collect company context

Own the canonical company reference: engagement goal and baseline, stable customer ICP, and selection and definition of three broad engagement target tracks—ICP #1, ICP #2 and Connectors. Help the user choose audiences most likely to advance the goal; hypotheses, candidates, review batches and iteration learning belong to `daily-opportunities`.

Start from the customer page or research-partner directory linked in the conversation or workspace instructions. Resolve the customer and connected noticed team from available context; ask only when ambiguous.

Before writing company context, resolve the canonical partner record. When a directory is available, check for a matching partner and reuse it; if absent, create it in that directory using its existing/default partner template, discovered from the directory. Never use a standalone/private staging page in its place. Keep this workspace setup out of the company reference.

1. Read [company-context-template.md](company-context-template.md) and available company materials; research material gaps before interviewing. Establish the current strategy, available offer and engagement goal. Prioritize current, direct evidence and sources the user identifies as authoritative. Describe only currently available customer-facing offers and pricing. Resolve material conflicts with the user; present a provisional understanding without asking them to repeat known information.

2. Ask one focused question at a time when the answer materially changes targeting, fit, approach or success measurement. Establish the meeting target, deadline and relevant business goal and baseline before validation. Prefer concise, grounded options labeled with numbers or letters; say whether one or several can be chosen and allow free-form corrections. Use examples when helpful or an open question when choices would be invented. After each answer, ask the next necessary question or present the finished draft for validation. Saving progress is not a stopping point; resolve material gaps in conversation rather than parking them under “To clarify.”

3. Make target-profile selection the main discussion. Propose several distinct options, offering a real choice beyond the three final slots. Label each option with a number or letter. For each, briefly describe who they are, the situation that makes them relevant, why they could advance the goal, and the supporting evidence or assumptions. Include direct audiences and connectors; a connector option should explain whom they could reach. Let the user pick winners by label, combine or reject options, and optionally explain their choices. Ask for reasoning only when it would materially improve the profiles. Refine the options together instead of choosing on the user’s behalf.

4. Confirm and save meaningful definitions for the three selected target profiles. ICP tracks may represent customers, investors or another audience relevant to the goal. The stable customer ICP describes the company’s durable best-fit customers; the engagement tracks are audiences being tested and need not put that ICP in ICP #1. If the user has not chosen all three, retain the confirmed choices and surface the remaining decision; do not invent agreement to fill a slot. Approval means worth pursuing, not proven fit.

5. Fill the reference using the companion template. Research and provisional profiles may be saved incrementally on the canonical page, clearly marked draft/unvalidated until explicit reference validation. Distinguish capabilities from plans, results from targets, and facts from assumptions. Date baselines and time-sensitive claims. Aim for 600–800 words containing only information that changes targeting, fit, approach or success measurement. Capture the team’s existing approach and preferences; leave recommended outreach tactics, network selection and execution decisions to later routines. Do not pad missing information.

6. Once material questions and conflicts are resolved—or the user explicitly accepts an unknown—save and present the actual draft for validation, especially the goal, current offer, stable ICP and selected profiles. Incorporate corrections and obtain explicit validation; profile selection alone does not validate the document. Set **Last reviewed** only on validation; unvalidated revisions remain drafts. Read back the saved content to verify it matches the agreed reference, then confirm completion with its link.

For Notion, use **Knowledge → Company context**, with each section inside a toggle. Follow renamed sections by their meaning; preserve human edits and computed properties. Keep supporting materials and source links in **Library**, avoiding repeated links throughout the reference. The companion template defines the content independently of its destination.

This is the current company reference, not a learning log. Keep customer-specific information outside this skill.
