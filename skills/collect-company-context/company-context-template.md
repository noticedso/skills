## Company

- Name, website, what the company does, and whom it helps.
- Relevant stage, geography and operating context.

## Product & offer

- Problem solved, current offer, customer value and business model.
- Current customer-facing pricing and delivery capabilities when relevant; distinguish available features from plans. Exclude obsolete offers and internal pricing options.

## Market & positioning

- Market category, conditions and changes that affect the company.
- Competitors and alternatives, including existing workflows or doing nothing.
- Differentiation supported by evidence or explicitly stated as company positioning.

## ICP & target profiles

### Stable customer ICP

- The company’s durable best-fit customers: organization, buyer/user and core need.
- Confirmed poor fits and actual examples, distinguishing customers from prospects or desired customers.

### Selected target profiles

Record the three audiences being tested for this engagement: **ICP track 1**, **ICP track 2**, and **Connector track**. Direct tracks can serve goals such as sales or fundraising; they do not redefine the stable customer ICP, which need not occupy ICP track 1.

For each selected profile:

- **Who:** role, organization characteristics and relevant situation; for connectors, whom they can reach.
- **Why:** how they could advance the goal and why the opportunity could matter to them.
- **Basis & boundaries:** supporting evidence, main exclusions and untested assumptions.

Label unconfirmed choices as proposed. Keep the final reference focused on the winners; matching hypotheses, candidate lists and iteration history live in the opportunity routine.

## Business goals & baseline

- Priority outcome, meeting target and deadline, with a clear success measure.
- Dated starting point, current results and material constraints.
- Distinguish the company’s business goal from the engagement’s contribution to it.

## Sales approach

- How the team currently pursues the goal: channels, tactics and process.
- What works, what does not, and known bottlenecks.
- For other goals, describe the relevant fundraising, hiring or partnership approach. Record existing practices and preferences; leave proposed outreach tactics and network selection to later routines.

## Team & responsibilities

- User’s role and relevant team members, responsibilities and decision-makers.
- Link known profiles where useful.
