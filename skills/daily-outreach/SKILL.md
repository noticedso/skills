# Daily outreach

Turn an explicit human Yes into outreach while preserving attribution. Read the
[research-partner workflow](../_shared/research-partner-workflow.md), the Company
Brain, segment, source list, noticed relationship, and existing Outreach entry.

## Choose the opportunity

Use the person selected by the user. If none is selected, suggest one approved
person who needs outreach and ask whether to proceed. List membership alone is not
approval.

Reuse the existing Outreach entry for the same person and goal. Set Source list to
the batch that motivated the outreach; keep evidence from other lists in History
& source. Reconcile confirmed sends, replies, bookings, and drops before drafting.

## Develop the message

Recommend a direct message or introduction, sender, channel, and angle from the
goal, company offer, segment evidence, relationship history, and real connections.
Ask one focused question only when a material choice cannot be inferred.

Follow the outreach voice and first-message rules in the Company Brain's Sales
process & channels section. Draft one short recommended message. Offer alternatives when there is a real
decision to compare. For email, include a subject. For an introduction, separate
the request to the intermediary from any forwardable text. Never invent
familiarity, relationships, or company facts.

## Save and record

Use the live Outreach template defined in the workflow contract. Save the chosen
copy, concise fit rationale, evidenced introduction route, and Source list.
Preserve human edits.

A human sends the message. Set Sent on and Contacted only after a confirmed send.
Record replies, accepted meetings, and drops from evidence. An intermediary's
reply is not the target's reply; a proposed time is not a booking.

Use Source list → Goal for the correct Outreach and Meetings metrics. Read back
the entry, then report what was saved, the confirmed outcome, and the next
responsible person. Do not contact anyone or book a meeting without separate
authorization.
